Wall Inspection Symptoms

MODERATE RISK • STABILIZATION REQUIRED

Active Lateral Soil Pressure

Inward deflection between 1 and 2 inches indicates that external soil and hydrostatic pressure has overcome the tensile strength of the mortar joints. Wall cannot self-correct and will continue to displace.

Recommended Repair Protocol:

Carbon Fiber Structural Straps or Carbon Fiber Grid System bonded with high-tensile epoxy to halt inward bowing permanently.
