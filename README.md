## 🚨 Forensic Executive Summary: The 3 Fatal Vulnerabilities

Our live crawler audited all 31 reachable endpoints on `foundationrepairprosofjoplin.com` and revealed **three critical structural flaws** suppressing their local Joplin search visibility:

1. **Severe Geographic Mismatch (Bolivar, MO):**
   - The homepage `<title>` is configured as `"Foundation Repair in Bolivar, MO"` and the primary H1 is `"#1 Foundation Repair in Bolivar, Missouri"`.
   - Bolivar is located **90 miles northeast** of Joplin. This catastrophic geo-mismatch completely confuses Google's local indexing algorithms and suppresses Local Map Pack placement for high-intent queries like *"foundation repair joplin mo"*.
2. **External CTA Lead Bleed:**
   - Homepage conversion buttons (`"Get Estimate"`, `"Contact Our Team"`) link directly to `https://bolivarfoundationrepair.com/...` with `target="_blank"`. Joplin homeowners seeking local foundation bids are redirected away from the business, directly losing revenue.
3. **Broken XML Sitemaps & Staging Exposure:**
   - The primary sitemap (`local-sitemap.xml`) returns an HTTP 404 origin error.
   - An unfinished internal test page (`/google-setup/`) is indexed publicly in Google search results.
